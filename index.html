Mouse & keyboard or touch (iPad). Requires a browser with WebGPU. Sound recommended.

How to play

Desktop

  • Left-click a Marine to select his team · drag a box to select several · Shift adds
  • Right-click ground or an enemy for the order menu · right-drag, WASD or arrows pan · wheel zooms
  • Hotkeys: M move, R fast, C sneak, B retreat, F fire, G assault, Q smoke, E defend, Z ambush, U unload, X stop · Space pause · Tab next team · T elevation view

iPad / touch

  • Tap a Marine or a unit card to select · drag to pan · pinch to zoom
  • Tap ground or an enemy with a team selected for the order menu
  • Long-press another team to add it to the selection
  • Or pick an order on the bar, then tap the target
  • ⛰ in the top bar toggles the elevation view (contours and spot heights)

Tactics

  • Fire suppresses. Suppressed men slow down, pinned men stop and hug the ground, cowering men do nothing. Bring morale back by getting out of the line of fire.
  • Cover is real: the seawall, craters, trenches and wrecked amtracs stop bullets. Men behind a wall must rise to fire over it.
  • Pillboxes and bunkers can only see through their embrasure. Suppress the embrasure from the front, work engineers round the blind side, then Assault — flamethrower ("blowtorch") and satchel charge ("corkscrew").
  • Higgins boats ground on the reef at low tide. Amtracs cross it but can't climb the seawall.
  • Naval gunfire and air strikes need a team with a working radio ashore. Keep your command post alive.
